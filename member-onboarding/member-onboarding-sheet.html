Taking a seat on the Verana Council

What a seat means, what it asks of your organization, how the network pays for itself, and the steps to take it.

One status
One seat, one vote, one validator node
Size
25 seats maximum
Cost of membership

Mainnet, Q1 2027

The role

  • The Council is the only body that governs and secures the live Verana network. It is separate from the Verana Foundation (specifications, open-source software, token).
  • Each member holds one seat and one vote and operates one validator node. Seats span sectors and regions.
  • Members decide by a two-thirds majority of seated members: the governance frameworks, network parameters and budgets, software upgrades, network-level sanctions and admissions.
  • The Council does not write standards, sell products, give grants, issue the token or govern other ecosystems.

What your organization receives

  • A vote on the rules of an open public trust infrastructure, with governments, standards bodies, enterprises and academia.
  • Free membership: no dues, no capital contribution, and no liability for the association's debts.
  • After launch, a node budget sized to cover the cost of running the validator (see below).
  • A complimentary Verana Foundation Associate Membership.

What a seat does not carry

  • No token allocation, no revenue share, no economic claim of any kind. Token holdings give no vote.
  • No extra power for Board members: the Board holds no network authority.

Obligations of a member

FormationCo-author the governance frameworks. Vote in 14-day asynchronous ballots. Run a testnet validator node (recommended) to be ready for genesis.
From mainnetOperate one validator node. This is mandatory: votes are cast through it. A member not ready at launch keeps its seat and joins within 90 days.
OperationsAvailability of at least 95% over any 30 days (target 99.5%). Adopted upgrades applied within 14 days. Keys kept separate and in the member's own custody.
ReportingSuspected key compromise within 24 hours. Material incident within 72 hours. Change of hosting or operator within 5 business days. Change of control within 14 days.
PeopleOne voting representative. Two emergency governance contacts able to vote on an urgent security proposal, reachable at all times, tested quarterly.
NodeAGPL-3.0-or-later software; modified builds publish their source. Own data center, colocation or cloud, disclosed publicly. A contracted operator is allowed; the member stays fully accountable.
ConductStatutes and Code of Conduct. Recusal on conflicts of interest. One seat per corporate group. Seatings, votes and departures are public.
Term and exit3-year terms with formal renewal. Orderly handover on departure. Breaches follow a graduated ladder, from warning to removal for cause, with due process and appeal.

Budget and distribution

Every fee paid on the network enters a single pool. Once a day the protocol pays the pool out in a fixed order:

1
Validator node budgets
The same amount for every active node, reduced when uptime is low
2
Council budget
Runs the association; accounts published every year
3
Foundation budget
Paid to the Foundation's treasury
4
Yield on bonded tokens
Capped; not active at launch
5
Remainder
Burned, never redistributed

What a member is paid. The node budget is a cost target set in fiat currency and paid in the native token (VNA). It is paid in full when the node signs at least 99% of the day's blocks, pro-rated below that, and not paid under 90%.

Who sets the amounts. The budgets are set at genesis, then changed only by a two-thirds Council vote with a published justification, and reviewed in public every year. The amounts are not set yet.

No new tokens. Supply is fixed at 1 billion VNA and only shrinks. While fees are below the budgets, nodes are paid first and the shortfall comes from the founding treasuries and vesting grants.

Onboarding checklist

1Name an internal sponsor and confirm who can represent the organization.
2Submit the non-binding expression of interest on veranacouncil.org (sector and region). Nothing is signed yet.
3Vetting by the Membership & Seats Committee.
4Admission ballot: two-thirds of seated members, 14 days. The first three seats are designated by the steward.
5Legal review of the Council Membership Agreement, the single binding document, then authorized signature.
6Designate the voting representative and the two emergency governance contacts.
7Choose own hosting or an operator, then start a testnet validator node (4+ CPU cores, 32 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD, static IP).
8Join the framework co-authoring and the Incorporation General Assembly, which ratifies all seatings.

Status. The Verana Council Association is a non-profit Swiss association (Verein, Art. 60 ZGB) in formation. Figures are initial parameters of the Network Governance Framework, draft 0.4 (Verifiable Public Registry specification, version 5), final once the Incorporation General Assembly ratifies it. This sheet is informative; the Council Membership Agreement and the statutes prevail.
